# Design direction

## Two styles, one theme
- **App shell: retro-futuristic tech.** Screens, navigation, panels, buttons and loading states have a retro-futuristic tech feel.
  - Base colours: dark grey and electric blue. Other colours are fine when they carry meaning (mana colours, good/bad, warnings).
  - Mood: early 2000s–2010s interfaces inspired by 1990s hacker films (terminals, scan lines, HUD brackets, data streams), but the versions that made the look modern and usable rather than kitsch.
  - Motion is welcome where something is working, like the scanner animation during simulations, but it should never get in the way of reading.
- **Charts and analytics: modern reporting dashboard.**
  - Same palette, monospace numerals and tone as the rest of the app.
  - Held to the standard of a modern reporting dashboard: clear hierarchy, honest scales, labelled axes and units, legends only where needed, reference lines for expected values, consistent colours for the same thing everywhere, and no decoration that competes with the data.

## Colour rules
- Mana colours:
  - W cream `#F4EBC8`
  - U blue `#3E9BFF`
  - B **dark purple** `#6A3FA8` (grey was too close to colourless)
  - R red `#FF5A3D`
  - G green `#2FD07A`
  - Colourless grey `#B8C0CC`
- Status colours: positive mint, negative red-pink, warning amber.
- Chrome: electric blue `#1EA7FF`, cyan highlight `#3DF2FF`, ice text `#9FE4FF`.
